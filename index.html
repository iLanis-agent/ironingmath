Ironing math

The basket never empties, the iron hums longer than anyone admits, and nobody has ever priced a Sunday at the board. Ironing math turns the pile into minutes and hours, the iron's wattage into kilowatt-hours and a yearly bill, and the backlog into sessions that actually fit the week. Exact arithmetic, every borrowed number labeled.

Open the calculators

The three tools

  • Pile plan - items and labeled minutes each become the time at the board.
  • Energy plan - the iron's wattage and minutes become kWh, a session's cost and a year of ironing.
  • Schedule plan - the pile in minutes becomes sessions and weeks to clear.

Labeled norms

Labeled pace: 5 minutes a shirt, 4 for simple items - illustrative, since fabric and standards move the number. Labeled iron: 2000 W. Heat settings belong to the garment label, never to a calculator - check the care symbols on each item.
